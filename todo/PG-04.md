## PG-04 — Geo data viewer on OpenStreetMap

- **Status:** todo
- **Depends on:** PG-01

### Goal

Geometry/geography values are unreadable as WKB hex in a grid. Render them on a
map instead.

### Acceptance criteria

- [ ] PostGIS `geometry`/`geography` columns are detected (and the absence of
      PostGIS handled without error).
- [ ] A grid cell holding a geometry shows a readable summary (type, SRID, point
      count) plus a control to open it on a map.
- [ ] A map view renders results using OpenStreetMap tiles, supporting points,
      lines, polygons and multi-* collections.
- [ ] Selecting a feature on the map highlights its row, and vice versa.
- [ ] Non-4326 SRIDs are transformed for display, or clearly reported as
      untransformable.
- [ ] Sane limits: a cap on rendered features with a "showing N of M" notice.
- [ ] Tile usage respects OSM's tile policy (attribution shown, sensible caching);
      the tile URL is configurable so users can point at their own server, and
      map rendering is disabled when the app is offline rather than hanging.

### Out of scope

Editing geometries.
