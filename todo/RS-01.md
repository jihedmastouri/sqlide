## RS-01 — Research: improve the Query Builder

- **Status:** done — research complete, follow-ups filed

### Questions to answer

- What do people actually use a visual query builder for — exploration, or
  producing SQL they then edit by hand?
- Round-tripping: can builder → SQL → builder survive hand edits, or is it
  one-way? (This is the decision that shapes everything else.)
- Joins: inferred from foreign keys, or manual? Multi-table, self-joins,
  subqueries, CTEs, window functions — where do we draw the line?
- Where does it live: its own tab, or a panel attached to the query console?
- Survey two or three competitors and write down what's good and bad about each.
